How to play

Each player starts with 1 card. Play goes clockwise. On your turn, announce a poker hand stricter than the last one — or call Trombón! if you don't believe it.

The hand is checked across all players' cards combined. If the hand exists, the caller loses. If it doesn't, the announcer loses.

The loser gets +1 card the next round. At 5 cards, getting burnt eliminates you. Last player standing wins.
